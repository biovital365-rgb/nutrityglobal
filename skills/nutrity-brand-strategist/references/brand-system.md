# Nutrity brand-system framework

Use this reference for brand strategy, visual direction and manual creation.

## Strategic foundation

Document concise answers to:

- Audience: who arrives from BioVital.360, what they are trying to improve and what creates skepticism.
- Category: the frame Nutrity should own. Prefer “education and guided habit support” unless evidence justifies another category.
- Positioning: for whom, what useful outcome, how the product helps and why it is credible.
- Promise: a benefit Nutrity can consistently deliver through the product experience.
- Reasons to believe: visible product capabilities, qualified human support, transparent sources and observable actions.
- Personality: three to five traits with behavioral definitions, not isolated adjectives.
- Brand idea: one memorable organizing thought that can guide both design and language.

## Identity territories

Each proposed territory should include:

- a short name and one-sentence concept;
- audience emotion and intended perception;
- symbol and wordmark direction;
- palette logic, typography character, imagery and graphic behavior;
- application sketch for TikTok handoff, landing page and dashboard;
- differentiation, risks and accessibility considerations.

Evaluate territories for distinctiveness, relevance, credibility, flexibility, reproduction, accessibility and ownership risk. A trademark search is a separate legal workstream and must not be implied by visual review.

## Logo system

Define primary horizontal, compact, symbol-only and monochrome variants when justified. Specify clear space with a measurable unit, minimum sizes for screen and print, background rules and prohibited distortions. Export master SVG/PDF plus production SVG, PNG and favicon assets. Keep all production assets local and versioned.

## Color and typography

Define color roles rather than an ornamental list: brand primary, accent, surface, text, success, warning, danger and data visualization. Provide HEX/RGB and, when print is required, CMYK/Pantone approximations subject to proofing. Verify WCAG contrast for UI pairs.

Define display, heading, body, label and numeric styles. Include licensed sources or safe fallbacks and rules for Spanish punctuation, accents and responsive sizing.

## Priority applications

Validate the system on:

1. BioVital.360 TikTok profile/link transition.
2. Nutrity landing hero and plan selection.
3. Onboarding and medical-safety notice.
4. Dashboard navigation and Ruta Nutrity.
5. Academia lesson/unit.
6. Transactional email and educational PDF.

The relationship should read: “BioVital.360 te conecta con Nutrity Global”, never as two competing product brands.
