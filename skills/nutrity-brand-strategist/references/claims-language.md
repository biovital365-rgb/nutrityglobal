# Health-related brand language

Use this reference for public copy, offer naming, taglines and educational content.

## Preferred territory

Write about actions and product capabilities that are observable:

- organizar hábitos;
- comprender información;
- registrar avances;
- preparar preguntas para un profesional;
- construir una rutina sostenible;
- recibir orientación educativa y acompañamiento.

Use “persona”, “miembro” or “usuario” for the consumer product. Reserve “paciente” for a real professional-care relationship and its administrative context.

## Avoid or qualify

Do not use unqualified promises such as:

- cura, revierte, remite o elimina una enfermedad;
- salva vidas;
- estabiliza o controla la glucosa;
- evita picos de insulina;
- previene enfermedades;
- garantiza resultados;
- diagnóstico, prescripción o tratamiento personalizado;
- análisis de biomarcadores when the product only stores user-entered data.

Replace absolute outcomes with educational, behavior-based language. Examples:

| Avoid | Prefer |
| --- | --- |
| “El orden que salva vidas” | “Una forma práctica de organizar tus comidas” |
| “Estabiliza tu glucosa” | “Observa cómo tus hábitos se relacionan con tus registros” |
| “Previene enfermedades crónicas” | “Construye hábitos que apoyen tu bienestar” |
| “Metodología clínica para acelerar resultados” | “Ruta educativa para sostener cambios posibles” |
| “Analizamos tus biomarcadores” | “Organizamos la información que registraste” |

Disclaimers do not rescue a misleading headline. Correct the main claim first, then add a concise limitation where it helps safe use.

## Brand voice

- Clear: explain one useful idea at a time.
- Warm: support without infantilizing or dramatizing.
- Credible: distinguish evidence, suggestion and personal choice.
- Active: invite a small next action.
- Calm: use urgency only for genuine safety escalation.

Avoid fear, shame, exaggerated transformation, medical authority theater and dense scientific decoration.
